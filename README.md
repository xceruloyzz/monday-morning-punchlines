Entry for the [Monday Morning Punchlines](https://make.fun/c/u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc) bounty on make.fun — one joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters, judged on laughs.

## The entry

> Monday: my alarm rings, my coffee quits, and my bed files for custody of me.

(76 characters, one line.)

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) — deterministically, directly to the final file with no temp files and no trailing newline, after validating it is non-empty, a single line, and within the 200-character gate.
